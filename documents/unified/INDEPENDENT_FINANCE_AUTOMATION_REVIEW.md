## Scope and financial contracts

The review followed cancellation through calendar readback, its durable financial plan, refund submission/recovery, account-owned credit grants, replacement deposit and balance allocation, signed payment events, onsite collection, Billing views, and the existing reconciliation worker. It also reviewed the credential bridge, source reversal, exact predecessor compatibility and code-rollback guard.

The implemented policy matches the confirmed scope: management cancellation refunds collected cash payments regardless of notice; customer cancellation at least 24 hours before the confirmed appointment refunds the paid deposit; later customer cancellation grants deposit credit; additional customer prepaid cash goes to staff review. Replacement credit funds the deposit first and then the balance, retains unused value, and is restored rather than issued again when that replacement is cancelled. Existing cancelled orders are not automatically backfilled.

Gross prices and existing payment identities remain separate from the new cash/credit ledger. Fully credited deposits do not invent Stripe sessions or payment intents. Later card collection uses verified source-card lineage and fresh consent; the original cash-deposit consent remains compatible. Credit authorization is based on durable account ownership and the current payment capability, with lock-time checks following provider reads. Vendor authorization and the displayed final scope are rechecked before additional balance credit is reserved.

Refund requests retain one exact body and idempotency key. Unknown responses are recovered by exact refund metadata or saved refund identity; aged unknown submissions do not receive a fresh POST. Succeeded refunds require matching TEST charge readback. Existing adjustments and disputes require review. The worker uses the existing account-owned scheduler and a private TEST credential bridge; credentials are not put in arguments or logs. Calendar and notice recovery retain their existing provider identities and guarded operations.

## Resolved findings

The paths below identify the corrected implementation, rather than an outstanding defect.

- **High — late signed payment could be stranded by stale completion.** In `_private/server/booking-finance.php`, processing initially read paid balances, performed refund provider calls, then updated the plan to completed without a final write transaction. A signed balance event arriving during those calls could queue the plan and then have that wake-up overwritten. An independent actual-module harness injected an $80 balance event during refund readback for a $50 management cancellation: the old result was completed with only $50 refunded and zero plans eligible for the worker. The corrected final `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` reselects current paid balance rows, refund states, open attempts, owner/job identity and other applied cancellations before updating the plan. The same independent harness now leaves the first pass pending and eligible for the worker; the next pass refunds the full $130 and completes.
- **High — credit and cash amounts could be counted inconsistently across collection and recovery.** `_private/server/booking-job.php`, `booking-job-catalog.php`, `portal-billing.php` and `booking-finance.php` now use the separate verified cash amount and actual used/reserved credit consistently. The final scope supports later approved services, returns unused reservations, and preserves the original gross bill. Restored deposit credit remains visible as historical cancellation evidence. Paid webhook replays do not spend restored credit again.
- **High — authorization could change during provider reads.** `booking_finance_reserve`, `booking_finance_credit_checkout` and `booking_finance_prepare_balance` now recheck account/capability or caller authorization under the write lock. Independent race probes and retained regressions cover disabled accounts, revoked payment capabilities and revoked Vendor grants, preserving wallet and job state on refusal.
- **High — cancellation or payment recovery could reuse the wrong financial state.** `booking_finance_queue` and `booking_finance_process` retain the original cancellation operation/policy and stop repeated applied cancellations for review. Signed late balance evidence queues the original financial plan inside the payment transaction. Exact unpaid credit-checkout expiry restores held credit and retires the old reduced-cash order, preventing its reuse.
- **Medium — exhausted credit sources could block unrelated purchases.** An independent harness showed a wallet with zero available credit blocking a new cash-only order after a fully spent historical source received a payment adjustment. Reservation now returns before source reads when available credit is zero; new deposit and balance allocation inspect only grants with positive remaining value. Used allocations still require their own source evidence when the affected booking is verified. Regressions also cover healthy available credit alongside an exhausted adjusted grant.
- **Medium — financial recovery could be delayed by notice/provider ordering or the worker budget.** Staff, account and signed management cancellation callers now attempt the saved customer notice before synchronous financial processing. The financial plan is already queued atomically with the verified cancellation, so interruption remains recoverable. The private TEST worker credential is provisioned before an authorized calendar cancellation can become uncertain. Financial processing has its own worker time budget rather than inheriting the elapsed calendar-reconciliation budget.
- **High — builder and installer predecessor pins initially differed.** `tools/build-unified-release.py` and `tools/install-unified.py` now agree on the installed c402 predecessor, its actual manifest hash and its original runtime schema. The release fixture rebuilds that predecessor using its own archived builder and validates the published package hash before importing its installer and release record. The new financial tables have an explicit additive runtime declaration. Any row in any of the five financial tables prevents restoration of old code; operational financial data requires repair forward.
- **Medium — historical finance reversal initially omitted its before-hash check.** `tests/historical-source.php` now requires strict base64 decoding and the exact before hash after validating the current reviewed after hash. The Python source chain retains both byte/hash guards and rejects unknown edits. All 18 financial source entries were independently compared with exact c402 Git objects and current application bytes.
- **Medium — a legacy unit test could fall through to the real billing transport.** `tests/portal-service.test.php` now supplies an explicit fail-closed financial transport. Four disposable browser copies rename the refund boundary and use a synthetic refund stub. The browsers already disabled outbound PHP transport functions; the new stub removes fallback into the production refund implementation. Production transport guards were unchanged and original role, consent, payment, calendar and recovery assertions were retained.
