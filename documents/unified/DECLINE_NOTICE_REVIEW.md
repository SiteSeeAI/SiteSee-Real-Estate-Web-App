# Customer decline notice correction (TEST)

The installed Booking Review correctly declines a customer's proposed arrival window and keeps the confirmed appointment, but its decline handler does not queue a customer email. The customer can see the declined request only in Account.

The correction records a plain customer decline notice in the existing communications outbox in the same SQLite transaction as the manager's decision. After commit, the explicit staff action submits that saved message through the existing dedicated sender and approved TEST recipient/CRM guards. It includes the requested window, the confirmed window at the time of decline and a link to Account for current status. It contains no calendar attachment, RSVP or cancellation. The separate escaped HTML representation preserves the same text for CRM history.

Calendar/event identities, confirmed booking and financial fields, the customer's original invitation and other appointment operations remain unchanged by decline. A notification failure does not undo the decision. Missing prerequisites retain a prepared notice; a lost draft/send response remains uncertain and cannot cause automatic resubmission. Explicit staff recovery verifies the saved sent copy, available recipient-copy evidence and existing CRM association. Only a verified unsent draft with no prior send attempt may continue under its original provider ID. External inbox receipt is never inferred from a sent copy.

Manage Appointment displays notice status and conditional Send/Check actions. Overview directs staff to unresolved notices, including older notices still needing recovery after a newer request. All notice actions retain staff authentication, CSRF, same-origin and fresh send consent checks. Customer pages expose no send/recovery actions.

The earlier version created no outbox record for a decline. Rendering or installing this correction does not send historical notices. Staff may explicitly prepare and send the latest saved declined request only while its original appointment fingerprint still matches. This lets the current acceptance booking receive its missing notice without repeating the appointment request. Missing older/stale notices require direct customer contact. Existing saved notices remain immutable; delayed sends label their window as historical and direct the customer to current Account status.
