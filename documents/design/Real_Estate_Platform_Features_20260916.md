# Real Estate Platform Features

2026 09 16

## Purpose

Agents, buyers and tenants need to discuss a property and retain the questions that arise during review. This update explains how in-platform meetings and location-based notes support that work within the broader SiteSee platform.

## Website Changes

The Platform page now has six capability sections. Its meeting section explains live property review with remote buyers, co-buyers and tenants, including an initial shortlist review and participation by a decision-maker who cannot attend in person. The new Property Notes & Team Collaboration section explains location-based questions, supporting attachments, shared context and question history through residential and commercial examples.

Connected property media, floor plans, AI decluttering/redesign and engagement remain part of the complete platform story. No new pricing, usage allowance or seat allocation is established by this copy update.

The homepage's four capability rows now link to the corresponding Platform sections. Services Provided links directly to meetings and notes in its platform introduction. All six page footers include In-Platform Meetings and Property Notes & Collaboration links. The existing meeting anchor remains `platform.html#online-meetings`; the new notes anchor is `platform.html#property-notes`.

The existing two-column design and local brand assets are retained. Notes uses the approved image-placeholder treatment pending an actual product image. The engagement row alternates after the new notes row. No product screenshot or client outcome has been fabricated.
