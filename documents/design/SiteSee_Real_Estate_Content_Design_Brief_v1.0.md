## Platform: Positioning and Page Order

The SiteSee platform is the central property presentation and evaluation environment. Its value comes from connecting the property, media, layout, possible changes, conversations, location-based notes and next actions. Online meetings are one important capability within that broader value.

The page gives each selected capability a short explanation and a practical real estate benefit. It does not reproduce the entire corporate feature catalogue or make meetings the hero message. Keep the same core platform proposition for individual agents, teams and brokerages; account capacity and access allocations vary with the plan.

### Meeting Allowance and Cost Basis

Interpret the user's proposed 45–60-credit allowance as a request for an included meeting allowance, not a confirmed currency or time conversion. Public HUB documentation meters meeting usage in minutes. The recommendation is 60 meeting minutes per paid property slot per month, pooled for the client account: 180, 420 or 720 minutes. Confirm how the user's legacy account labels and prices these units before finalizing the offer.

The official HUB FAQ lists 1,000 extra minutes at $20, equivalent to $0.02 per minute. On that published replacement-cost basis, 60 minutes costs $1.20; the three full monthly allowances cost $3.60, $8.40 and $14.40 respectively. These are meeting-usage figures only, not total hosting/support costs or a verified SiteSee legacy invoice.

Accepted additional customer allowance: 300 minutes (five hours) for $20. The implied published underlying minute cost is $6; actual account pricing, fees, administration and support must be checked. Do not promise unlimited meetings.

The provider assigns HUB minutes to a client/brand profile, making client-level pooling a logical fit. Its documentation says unused minutes roll over and calls end when the balance reaches zero. Minutes can continue to run after the host leaves if guests remain. Establish the monthly refill and any rollover cap in the actual client/account process before advertising automated resets, alerts or uninterrupted overage behavior. Teach hosts to end meetings for all participants. Purchased additional minutes can remain available while the customer's subscription is active, subject to the final agreed policy.

### Commercial Validation

These launch recommendations were accepted by the user. They have not yet been validated against SiteSee's actual hosting, licensing, AI generation, payment and support costs or customer willingness to pay. The dedicated Platform page strengthens the value proposition without changing the accepted price points. Corporate deployments have broader operational scopes and retain their separate pricing architecture.

As an illustrative margin check, a $449 monthly plan requires total recurring direct delivery costs at or below $134.70 to leave a 70% gross margin. At the published $0.02 meeting-minute benchmark, the 720-minute allowance accounts for $14.40 of that ceiling, leaving $120.30 for other direct recurring costs. Include allocated platform/base-model hosting, payment fees, support labor and other recurring delivery costs; do not treat included legacy minutes as a permanent zero-cost supply.

Start with monthly subscriptions and test the offer with initial customers before adding annual discounts. Track adoption, actual monthly active properties, meeting usage, support time and renewal behavior. A higher price should be earned by configuration, integrated media presentation and ongoing service quality.
