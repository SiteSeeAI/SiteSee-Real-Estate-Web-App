From First Selection To Finished Media

Give Prospects Useful Context Before They Visit.

Commercial buyers need more than an attractive exterior. They need to see how a property is arranged and how people, goods and vehicles move through it. We plan views of the building and working spaces, then use aerial imagery, floor plans, video or Matterport where those services help explain access, circulation and scale.

Your Project, Step By Step

A Straightforward Process.

  1. 01

    Choose Your Services

    Enter the property address and size, then add the media your marketing needs to the size-based photography estimate. Choose aerial-image quantities, video and floor-plan deliverables, and a usage term. The estimate separates Matterport hosting from photography licensing so the scope stays clear.

  2. 02

    Select Your Shoot

    Request a date and time that work for the property. We confirm access, occupied areas, site contacts and any escort or protective-equipment requirements before arrival. Clearing the planned views and arranging access helps avoid interruptions.

  3. 03

    Capture The Property

    We follow the agreed coverage plan through the building and exterior areas. Photographs establish the space and key features; selected aerials show the grounds and access. Video and Matterport add movement and layout context when they are part of your request.

  4. 04

    Prepare The Finished Media

    After capture, we organize and edit the material into a consistent collection. We prepare the selected photographs, video, layouts and experience for delivery and check that the media supports the property details you need to communicate.

  5. 05

    Receive Your Media Within 24 Hours

    Your finished property media is delivered within 24 hours of the completed shoot. Bring the collection into your listing, brokerage presentation or property website while interest is active and the marketing schedule is moving.

Usage & Hosting

Choose The Term Your Marketing Needs.

Choose a photography license from six to eighteen months. The fee is 30% of your still and aerial-image cost, divided by twelve and multiplied by your chosen number of months. Unlimited photography use adds 50% of that same image cost. Video rights are discussed separately. Choose finished media files, a property website or the SiteSee platform for delivery.

Matterport includes six months of hosting. A photography license does not extend hosting or provide unlimited Matterport use. Tell us if you need the experience available for longer so we can agree on the hosting term with you.
